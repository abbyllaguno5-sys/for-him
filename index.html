My Love Letter

My Lovelove,

Three years. It sounds like such a small number until I think about everything we've shared inside it. I wanted to build you something that wasn't just a card you'd read once and put in a drawer — something you could open again on a bad day and remember that you are loved.

Three years later, still falling in love with you. Many achievements, many cried moments, many challenges, many happy moments, many arguments, many things have changed, but I would still choose to be with you to face that roller coaster ride of emotions. I would rather fix all the arguments and accept your changes rather than end it.

Thank you, Love, for making the three years so bearable, for being so patient, for being consistent, for understanding me, for all the sacrifices and efforts you made — those are appreciated so much. I love you endlessly, my Lovelove!

Thank you for three years of being my favorite person. Here's to many more.

Always yours,
Lovey
